Marketing for an early-stage edtech building rigour into the way students prepare for everything that matters — translating product depth into language a parent and a teenager both trust.

The brief

What I walked into.

A serious product in a category drowning in hype. The mandate: position Stratos as the calm, credible choice without slipping into corporate flatness or test-prep cliché.

What I did

The shape of the work.

  • Wrote the positioning thesis and the editorial voice guide that everything else draws from.
  • Designed a parent–student dual-funnel content strategy across newsletter, social, and on-platform.
  • Built the launch campaign for the flagship cohort — long-form essay + capsule onboarding + a tight community thread.
  • Set up activation and retention measurement so the team argues with data, not opinions.
Receipts

What it earned.

—Sign-ups in the first launch window
—Activation lift after voice rewrite
—Newsletter open rate, sustained

What I'd do differently

The next time.
