The brief

What I walked into.

A clinically rigorous product whose communication still read like a research paper. The mandate: keep the rigour, lose the distance — and build a content engine that compounded.

What I did

The shape of the work.

  • Coached the founder into the front of camera; wrote the recurring column that anchored the newsletter.
  • Built the editorial voice guide and onboarded every new hire to it.
