I. Frameworks

How I think about marketing.

A handful of lenses I return to — borrowed from editors, brand-builders, and a few good books. Each is a way of asking better questions before the work begins.

I.

The Editorial Lens

Every brand has a magazine inside it. I look for the cover line, the masthead, the rhythm of what is said and what is left out — and treat the marketing calendar like an issue plan.

II.

Story before System

Funnels matter, but story sets the temperature. I write the narrative first — the why, the world, the voice — then design the system that carries it from headline to checkout.

III.

Proof & Patina

Trust is the new conversion lever. Reviews, press, founder presence, behind-the-scenes — I layer proof until the brand feels lived-in rather than launched.

IV.

Quiet Channels

Not every campaign needs paid media. Newsletters, partnerships, intimate events, and well-placed words often move further — and at a fraction of the cost.

V.

The Founder Voice

Audiences trust people more than logos. I ghost-write, coach, and stage the founder so the brand inherits their conviction without sounding rehearsed.

VI.

Measurable Restraint

Less, better, traceable. I prefer three campaigns done with care over twelve done in haste — and instrument every one so we can argue with data, not opinions.

II. Case Studies

Brands, in detail.

Three pieces of work that reflect how I move — from positioning, to launch, to the slower craft of building a brand that lasts.

Case 01 — Hospitality

A boutique hotel finds its voice.

Brand lead · 2024–2025

A heritage property in the old quarter of Dubai had the rooms, the chef, and the location — but no language. We rebuilt the brand from a single guiding line: arrive as a guest, leave as a regular. Every touchpoint, from booking confirmation to turndown card, was rewritten around it.

I led positioning, voice, the launch campaign, and the editorial cadence for the first twelve months. We replaced paid acquisition with a quieter mix — a print mini-zine for arriving guests, a founder column, partnerships with three independent perfumers and a Beirut florist.

Case 02 — Beauty

A skincare launch, written like a memoir.

Marketing lead · 2023–2024

A founder-led skincare line arrived in a category exhausted by claims. Instead of competing on actives, we made the founder the protagonist — her library, her clinic, her grandmother's recipes — and let the product appear in the margins.

The launch lived on three surfaces: a long-form essay, a private list of one thousand readers, and a single capsule of product. No discounts, no influencer seeding. We sold out the first run in seventy-two hours and built a waiting list that became the brand's most valuable channel.

Case 03 — Fintech

Translating a serious product into human language.

Brand consultant · 2022–2023

A wealth platform for first-generation investors had the product right and the tone wrong — earnest but stiff, careful but cold. We rebuilt the editorial voice around plain English, real numbers, and a refusal to perform expertise.

The homepage became an essay. The onboarding became a conversation. We launched a weekly letter, a glossary, and a small library of founder interviews. Activation climbed because the brand started sounding like the friend who actually knows finance.
