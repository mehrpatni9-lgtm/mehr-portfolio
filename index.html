I.A little about me

I work at the intersection of creativity, data, and execution — with a high bar for what counts as good marketing.

Most recently at Ivory, a health-tech app, I worked beside founders and clinicians to translate complex cognitive science into clear, human stories across product, content, and social. Decisions grounded in engagement, retention, and behaviour — but always guided by brand clarity and long-term positioning.

I think in systems, not just campaigns: how a brand shows up consistently, how moments ladder up to meaning, and how creativity moves the needle beyond vanity metrics.

Hands-on by instinct

Editing videos, shaping visuals, refining copy, pushing ideas until they feel right. I'm drawn to well-crafted campaigns, strong copy, thoughtful design, and distribution that does justice to the idea.

Previously, brand and copy work for Wahed and at IPG Media Brands — across regulated and fast-moving categories where trust, taste, and timing matter equally.

Endlessly curious about how people interact with design, what stories stick, and how creativity can quietly change behaviour.
