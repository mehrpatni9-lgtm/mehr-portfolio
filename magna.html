2026 — Present

Translating senior strategy intent into editorial work that compounds — founder briefings, original research, and a creative system the rest of the practice can run on.

The brief

What I walked into.

A strategy ecosystem with senior credibility, no editorial discipline, and a content output that was reactive at best. The mandate: turn the practice's intellectual capital into a brand that ages.

What I did

The shape of the work.

  • Built the editorial calendar around founder briefings — one long-form thesis a month, recut into the Rule-of-Five surfaces.
  • Established the visual system: typography, hairline grid, motion principles — every output legible across deck, social, and print.
  • Hired and ran a small studio of writers, designers, and a producer.
  • Quarterly review cadence with the partners; killed two-thirds of the briefs that didn't earn their place in the calendar.
Receipts

What it earned.

—Founder briefings published, year one
—Owned audience growth across surfaces
—Inbound qualified strategy briefs

What I'd do differently

The next time.

Start with the production discipline before the brand work. Voice is downstream of cadence — a calendar that ships is worth more than a manifesto that doesn't.
