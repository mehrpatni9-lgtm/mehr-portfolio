Ch. II · The format handbook

Different formats do different jobs.

Pick the format that does the job. Don't force a job into the wrong format.

A reel cannot do what a podcast does. A LinkedIn post cannot do what a YouTube long-form does. The fastest way to waste budget is to pretend they are interchangeable.

The next five pages are a quick handbook to the formats I keep coming back to, what each is uniquely good at, and a tight strategy for actually working in it.
