I.

Newsletter.

Trust · Weekly · Compounding

The highest signal-to-noise channel in your audience's life. Subscribed equals invited. Inbox is sacred.

Why it matters

A permission channel.

Algorithms decide who sees a post. The reader decides whether to open a newsletter. That tiny difference is everything, you are arriving as a guest, not as an ad.

The job it does

Long relationship maintenance.

Compound trust, one week at a time. The newsletter is where a stranger becomes a reader, a reader becomes a customer, a customer becomes a friend.

My strategy
  • One editorial spine. The reader should know what to expect within three issues.
  • Same day each week. Cadence is more important than length.
  • No growth tactics. The list is the asset; open rate is the symptom.
  • Personal voice. If a machine could have written it, don't send it.
Common mistakes
  • Treating it as a content recap. Newsletters are essays, not RSS feeds.
  • Buying subscribers. The list rots from the inside out.
  • Selling on every issue. The third one is the one that hurts.
"The quietest channel earns the loudest results."
