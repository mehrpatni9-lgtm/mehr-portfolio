Foreword · The thesis

Marketing that ages well.

A short opening note before the rest of the book.

Most marketing peaks the day it ships. A campaign goes live, the dashboard lights up, and three months later nobody remembers it ran. The work disappears at the speed of the algorithm.

I'm interested in the opposite. The deposits that compound. The voice that becomes infrastructure. The asset built like a campaign and earning forever after. I call it marketing that ages well, work built for the residue, not the peak.

This handbook is the shortest version of what I've learned writing for fintech, healthtech, and edtech brands across four markets. Five qualities that show up in every piece of work I respect. Five formats and the unique job each does. A few mantras I keep close.

It's not exhaustive. It's not academic. It's the kind of small book I'd hand a junior marketer on their first week.

Profit per piece, not views per piece.
