IV.

LinkedIn.

Presence · Daily · Decision-makers

The only feed where decision-makers actually browse during the workday. Distribution for the founder voice, at the room where serious business sits.

Why it matters

A boardroom, dressed as a feed.

CMOs read LinkedIn on the way into the meeting. Founders read it in line for coffee. The audience you are trying to reach is already here, scrolling.

The job it does

Founder voice distributed.

Daily presence by the founder is the cheapest inbound channel modern marketing has invented. Show up. Have a thesis. Repeat.

My strategy
  • Native long-form beats links out. The platform rewards readers, not clickers.
  • One thesis per post. No multi-topic carousels stitched together.
  • Daily cadence from the founder. Even three sentences. Especially three sentences.
  • No growth hacks. Hooks rot the voice. Earn it instead.
Common mistakes
  • Outsourcing the founder voice to a ghost-writer with no taste.
  • Re-posting press releases. The feed has lower tolerance for those than any other surface.
  • Trying to be funny. LinkedIn rewards conviction, not stand-up.
"Your CEO posts to one feed. Pick wisely."
