Ten lines I return to before I write anything. Print this page. Tape it above the desk.

i.
Profit per piece, not views per piece.
ii.
Boring beats viral when it compounds.
iii.
The deposits are the work. The withdrawal is the campaign.
iv.
Voice eats first. Tactics are the garnish.
v.
When everyone has the same machine — the taste behind it is the brand.
vi.
The quietest channel earns the loudest results.
vii.
Make it the answer to a question someone will ask in 2028.
viii.
An hour with you is worth fifty posts about you.
ix.
Reels make people feel. Carousels make people think.
x.
Pick two qualities before you ship. Pick four to be remembered.
