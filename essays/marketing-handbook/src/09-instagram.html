V.

Instagram.

Brand register · Visual · Cultural

Where the brand's register lives. Reels carry feeling, carousels carry ideas, and together they make a magazine.

Why it matters

A visual brand stack.

Instagram is where a stranger forms an opinion about how your brand looks before they know what it does. The grid is the brand cover.

The job it does

Top of funnel · social proof.

Brand recall at the surface, cultural fluency at the back. Use reels for energy, carousels for education, Stories for proof. Each does a different job.

My strategy
  • Reels = energy. Three seconds in, the audience should feel a register shift.
  • Carousels = ideas. Treat each slide like a page of a book, design for swiping.
  • Stories = proof. Where the messy real-time stuff lives.
  • The grid is the brand cover. Design for the third-tile reveal, not the single post.
Common mistakes
  • Cross-posting LinkedIn into Instagram. The audience is in a different mode.
  • Treating reels like ads. They get scrolled. Treat them like editorial.
  • Inconsistent grid covers. Visual whiplash kills the brand register.
"Reels make people feel. Carousels make people think."
