II.

YouTube.

Authority · Evergreen · Searchable

The only format where a single piece can work for a decade. Searchable, evergreen, founder-voice at distribution scale.

Why it matters

A library, not a feed.

Everything on YouTube is still discoverable five years later. That is unique in modern media. A good video pays its production cost back over years.

The job it does

Educational moat.

Build the answer to the question your category keeps asking. When the search exists, your video already answers it.

My strategy
  • Twelve to twenty minutes. Long enough to teach, short enough to finish.
  • Evergreen titles. No trend bait. Write the title someone will search in 2028.
  • The thumbnail and title are the entire pitch. The video is the keep.
  • One clear thesis per video. If you can't say it in one sentence, split the video.
Common mistakes
  • Re-cutting reels and calling it YouTube. The format is different; treat it different.
  • Chasing views over saves. Saves are the compounding metric.
  • Hiding the founder. The face on the thumbnail is the trust signal.
"Make it the answer to a question someone will ask in 2028."
