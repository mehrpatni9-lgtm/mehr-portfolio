III.

Podcast.

Intimacy · Slow · Parasocial

An hour of someone's attention is worth fifty posts about you. The podcast is the slowest, deepest channel — and the hardest to fake.

Why it matters

Parasocial trust at scale.

The listener spends sixty minutes with your voice in their head — on a run, in a car, washing dishes. No other format earns that proximity.

The job it does

Founder voice as relationship.

Two voices in conversation beat one talking head. Long arcs beat clip culture. Slow audience-building at depth no other channel can reach.

My strategy
  • Long conversations. Sixty to ninety minutes. The good part is at minute forty-four.
  • Don't edit your personality out. The pauses are part of the voice.
  • Two voices, not one. A guest sharpens the host.
  • Show notes are the search layer. Treat them like SEO copy.
Common mistakes
  • Twenty-minute episodes. Either commit to a long form or pick a different format.
  • Booking famous guests with thin questions. The questions are the brand.
  • Over-producing. Polish erases intimacy.
"An hour with you is worth fifty posts about you."
